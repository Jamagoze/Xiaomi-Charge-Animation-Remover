Xiaomi Charge Animation Remover

===============================



What it does:

Removes or disables the stock charging animation overlay on Xiaomi, Redmi, and POCO devices running MIUI and HyperOS.



Why use it:

- Stops the full-screen splash animation when plugging in a charger.

- Reduces UI delay/lag when connecting power.

- Keeps system behavior lightweight and clean.



Requirements:

- Root access (Magisk, KernelSU, or APatch)

- Xiaomi, Redmi, or POCO device on MIUI 13, MIUI 14, or HyperOS



Installation:

1. Download the latest release ZIP file.

2. Open your root app (Magisk).

3. Go to the Modules tab and select "Install from storage".

4. Pick the downloaded ZIP file.

5. Flash it and reboot your phone.



Uninstallation:

1. Open your root app.

2. Go to Modules, find this module, and tap Remove or Disable.

3. Reboot your device.



Disclaimer:

Use at your own risk. Always keep a backup before flashing root modules.
