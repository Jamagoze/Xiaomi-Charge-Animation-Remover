Information Security Policy

===========================



1. Purpose

This policy sets basic rules to protect company data, hardware, and accounts from unauthorized access or data loss.



2. Who This Applies To

All employees, contractors, and vendors using company devices, networks, or data.



3. Account & Password Rules

- Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on all accounts.

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.

- Use an approved password manager. Do not reuse passwords across personal accounts.

- Access is given based on the principle of least privilege (only what you need for your job).



4. Device Security

- Work must be done on company-managed devices.

- Laptops must have full disk encryption enabled.

- Lock screens automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

- Keep operating systems and software updated.



5. Network & Remote Work

- Connect via company VPN or secure network when working remotely.

- Do not use unsecured public Wi-Fi without a VPN.

- Keep screens private in public places.



6. Incident Reporting

Report any lost devices, suspicious emails, phishing attempts, or security breaches immediately to security@company.com.



7. Compliance

Failure to follow these security guidelines may result in disciplinary action up to termination.
